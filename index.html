Our Mission

Research for every undergraduate — not just the ones who already know the way.

ARISE exists to close the gap between curious students and the world of academic research. We believe the best ideas emerge when research is accessible to everyone — regardless of background, discipline, or prior experience.

Whether you're just getting started or thinking long-term about a research career, we're here to support every step of your journey at UTSC.

Our Values

What drives everything we do

01

Inclusivity

Research shouldn't be a privilege. We design every program to remove barriers — to be a first door, not a locked one.

02

Curiosity

The most important qualification for research is the willingness to ask a question you don't yet know the answer to.

03

Collaboration

The best research doesn't happen in isolation. We build bridges across disciplines, departments, and campuses.

04

Community

A space where students support each other — sharing resources, celebrating wins, navigating academia together.
