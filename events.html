Pumpkinstein: Building Up Thoughts

Ever wondered what it would be like to build Frankenstein from scratch… but with pumpkins? Join us for a spooky Pumpkin Painting Research Night, where we’ll bring Frankenstein to life while exploring the science behind the human body!

location_on SW 311, UTSC schedule 2 hours people Limited to 20 participants

What to expect

You’ll rotate through different stations focused on parts of the human body and their connection to research, all while enjoying yummy snacks! At each station, we’ll explore research papers, the importance of each body system, and how it connects back to the story and ideas of Frankenstein. And of course… you’ll be building your own Pumpkinstein!

Whether you’re into research, medicine, ethics, science, arts & crafts, or just want to make a spooky pumpkin, come join us!

Limited spots available. Maximum 20 participants!

Come create. Come research. Come bring the dead back to life.
